# Baldur's Gate 3

Released in August 2023 by Belgian studio Larian Studios, **Baldur's Gate 3** is a turn-based RPG built on the Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition ruleset. It is the long-awaited sequel to BioWare's beloved late-1990s Baldur's Gate games, set decades later in the Forgotten Realms.

The game shipped after three years in early access on PC, and its full release was unusually feature-complete — most studios cut content under deadline pressure, but Larian instead added more. Players can solve quests through dialogue, combat, stealth, or unexpected creativity (turning enemies into sheep, befriending villains, persuading gods). Almost every choice has voiced consequences and visible reactions from companions.

Baldur's Gate 3 won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2023, took the Best RPG award, and surprised the industry by also winning Game of the Year at the BAFTAs the following spring. It is widely credited with raising the bar for narrative reactivity and player agency in CRPGs, and is often discussed alongside Disco Elysium as a turning point for the genre.
