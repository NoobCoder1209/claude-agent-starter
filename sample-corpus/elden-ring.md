# Elden Ring

Released in February 2022 by FromSoftware, **Elden Ring** is the studio's first true open-world game and the breakout commercial hit of director Hidetaka Miyazaki's career. It pairs the punishing, deliberate combat of the Souls series with a vast, mysterious world co-written by *A Song of Ice and Fire* author George R. R. Martin.

The game won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2022 and sold more than 25 million copies in its first two years — extraordinary numbers for a studio previously known for cult-favourite niche titles. Critics praised the freedom of exploration, the density of secrets, and the way the world rewards patient curiosity rather than guided objectives.

In 2024, the *Shadow of the Erdtree* expansion shipped to similar acclaim and became one of the best-reviewed pieces of DLC ever released, cementing Elden Ring's place near the top of the modern RPG canon.
