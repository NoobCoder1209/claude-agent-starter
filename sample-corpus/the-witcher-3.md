# The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

Released in May 2015 by Polish studio CD Projekt Red, **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt** is the third and final mainline entry in the Witcher series, based on Andrzej Sapkowski's fantasy novels. The game follows Geralt of Rivia, a monster-hunting witcher, on a continent-spanning search for his adopted daughter Ciri.

The Witcher 3 reset expectations for what a story-driven open-world RPG could feel like. Side quests are written with the care normally reserved for main plots; minor characters carry weight; and player choices ripple through the world in ways that often have no clean "good" or "evil" answer.

Two major expansions, *Hearts of Stone* (2015) and *Blood and Wine* (2016), added dozens of hours of new content and are still cited as benchmarks for paid DLC. A free 2022 next-generation update introduced ray tracing and quality-of-life improvements, bringing the game to a new audience.

It has sold over 50 million copies and is widely considered one of the greatest games of the decade.
